Implemented:
- Trade ledger cycle detection on recorded zero-balance boundaries.
- Full-liquidation events reset price and fee cost bases to zero.
- Excess-sell or malformed entries listed as invalid rather than silently omitted.
- Potential duplicates (same date/side/qty/price) flagged, NEVER auto-deleted.
- Brokerage-reported holding reference compared with trade ledger.
- Saved predictions grouped by lookback, horizon, K, flat threshold.
- Show saved count, resolved count, directional accuracy, average simulated signal return, gross profit/loss ratio, simulated compounded MDD per parameter group.
- Existing backtest guide, tooltip and position strategy scenarios retained.

Limitations:
- Duplicate detection is a WARNING only (same-day identical fills can be valid).
- A missing closing trade cannot be inferred safely from other entries.
- Saved prediction verification occurs when historical price data resolves a prediction; scorecard is NOT a model retraining or calibrated probability estimator.
- Performance MDD is a simple sequential simulated signal calculation, not independently funded live trading, particularly when horizons overlap.
- End-to-end Windows/browser UI test not performed.

Safety:
- No automatic mutation of trades, saved predictions or account balances.
